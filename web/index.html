    How to play

    1. Each round a target colour is announced. Both players get 9 cards: 2, 3 and 4 cards of red / green / blue, in a random order of colours.
    2. On your turn you place 1 or 2 cards face-down and claim they are all the target colour. There is no passing.
    3. Instead of placing, you may call LIE on the other player's last play (not on the very first play of a round).
    4. The play is revealed. If it held any other colour, the liar loses a heart. If it was honest, the caller loses a heart.
    5. A player with no cards left must call. Every call ends the round. Zero hearts ends the match.

    Keys: R G B pick a card · Enter play · C call LIE · Esc clear · Space skip animations.

    Under the hood

    Four hand-made data structures run this game (no collections, heapq or queue). Each one is on screen.

    Linked list week 3
    Your hand. Cards stay sorted by colour. Playing a card from the middle re-points one pointer, no shifting. Remove: O(h).
    Stack week 4
    The pile. A call only looks at the newest play: last in, first revealed. Push, pop, peek: O(1).
    Max-heap week 8
    The bot scores every legal move and heapifies them, so the best is at the root. Build: O(c). Take the top few: O(K log c).
    Circular queue weeks 2 + 4
    The bot's memory of how you react: a sliding window of your last 8 answers. Enqueue and dequeue: O(1), no shifting.
    Recursion + memo
    The bot looks ahead through the rest of the round, caching each state it has already valued, so every state is computed once.
